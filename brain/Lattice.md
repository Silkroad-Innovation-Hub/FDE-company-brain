# Lattice

Lattice is [[Anduril]]'s AI-driven command-and-control platform — the connective tissue of the whole company. It ingests data from thousands of sensors and effectors, fuses it into a single real-time operating picture, and lets a small number of operators task large fleets of autonomous systems.

## Why it matters

- Every Anduril platform — [[Fury]], [[Ghost]], [[Roadrunner]], [[Barracuda]], [[Ghost Shark]], [[Anvil]], [[EagleEye]] — ships as a node on Lattice.
- The March 2026 [[Army Enterprise Contract]] centers Lattice as the backbone of the Army's counter-drone architecture, consolidating 120+ prior orders.
- Lattice for Mission Autonomy orchestrates collaborative behaviors: one operator, many machines.

## Business model implication

Hardware is the wedge; Lattice is the moat. Third parties can integrate via SDK, which turns competitors' sensors into Lattice feeds — see [[Competitors]] and [[Partnerships]].
