# Palmer Luckey

Founder of [[Anduril]] (2017). Previously founded Oculus VR at 19, sold to Facebook for ~$2B, and was pushed out in 2017 — then bet that Silicon Valley product velocity could out-build the primes.

- Public face of the company: recruiting, hill testimony, the "warfighter deserves better software" thesis.
- The [[IVAS]] takeover and [[EagleEye]] close a personal loop: the consumer VR founder shipping military mixed reality.
- Works alongside CEO [[Brian Schimpf]] — division of labor in [[Leadership]].
