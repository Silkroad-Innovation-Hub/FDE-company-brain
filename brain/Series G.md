# Series G — $2.5B at $30.5B (June 2025)

[[Anduril]]'s Series G closed in June 2025: **$2.5 billion at a $30.5 billion valuation**, at the time one of the largest private defense-tech rounds ever.

It bridged the company from program wins ([[IVAS]], [[CCA Program]]) into industrial scale-up at [[Arsenal-1]], and set up the doubling to [[Series H]] a year later. Context: [[Funding History]].
