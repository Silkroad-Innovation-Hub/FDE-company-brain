# Ghost Shark

Ghost Shark is an **extra-large autonomous undersea vehicle (XL-AUV)** co-developed with the Royal Australian Navy — Anduril's maritime flagship.

- Built on the Dive Technologies acquisition (Dive-LD / Dive-XL lineage) — see [[Acquisitions]].
- Delivered prototypes ahead of schedule and on budget; production contract with Australia followed.
- Long-endurance undersea ISR and strike, tasked through [[Lattice]].

Ghost Shark anchors the AUKUS/Indo-Pacific story in [[Partnerships]] and proves the model works outside the U.S.
