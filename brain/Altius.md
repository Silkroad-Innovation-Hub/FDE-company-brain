# Altius

Altius is a family of **tube-launched loitering munitions / UAS** that came with the Area-I acquisition (2021) — see [[Acquisitions]].

- Launches from ground vehicles, helicopters, and aircraft.
- Altius-600/700 variants carry ISR payloads or warheads.
- Swarm behaviors coordinated through [[Lattice]]; combat use in Ukraine.

Altius filled the strike gap between [[Ghost]] (ISR) and [[Barracuda]] (cruise missile) in the product ladder.
