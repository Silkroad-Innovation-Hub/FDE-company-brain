# EagleEye

EagleEye is the **mixed-reality soldier headset** line built with Meta — consumer-grade optics and compute hardened for the battlefield, running [[Lattice]] at the individual soldier level.

- Grew out of Anduril taking over the Army's [[IVAS]] program from Microsoft in February 2025.
- [[Palmer Luckey]] calls it the closing of a loop: the Oculus founder building military MR at scale.
- Turns every soldier into a Lattice sensor/effector node.

EagleEye is the consumer-electronics-supply-chain argument of [[Manufacturing Strategy]] applied to wearables.
