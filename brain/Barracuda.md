# Barracuda

Barracuda (announced 2024) is a family of **low-cost, software-defined cruise missiles** (Barracuda-100/250/500) built for affordable mass.

- Designed for automotive-style production: fewer parts, common tooling, commercial components.
- Solid rocket motor supply de-risked via the Adranos acquisition — see [[Acquisitions]].
- Production joins [[Arsenal-1]] alongside [[Fury]] and [[Roadrunner]] by end of 2026.
- Mission planning and in-flight retargeting run through [[Lattice]].

Barracuda is the munitions half of the [[Manufacturing Strategy]] argument: magazine depth wins long wars.
