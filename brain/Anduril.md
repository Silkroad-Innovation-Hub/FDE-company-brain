# Anduril Industries

Anduril is a defense technology company founded in 2017 by [[Palmer Luckey]], [[Brian Schimpf]], Matt Grimm, and Trae Stephens. Its core thesis: build **software-defined, mass-producible autonomous weapons** around [[Lattice]], the AI command-and-control platform that fuses sensor data and orchestrates fleets of unmanned systems.

As of mid-2026 the company is valued at **$61B** after its [[Series H]] round, with 2025 revenue of $2.2B and a projected $4.3B for 2026 — see [[Revenue]] and [[Funding History]].

## The system of systems

Every product connects back to [[Lattice]]:
- Air: [[Fury]] (collaborative combat aircraft), [[Ghost]] (ISR helicopter drone), [[Altius]] (loitering munitions), [[Roadrunner]] (reusable interceptor)
- Strike: [[Barracuda]] (cruise missile family)
- Maritime: [[Ghost Shark]] (autonomous submarines)
- Soldier: [[EagleEye]] (mixed-reality headset, built with Meta)
- Base defense: [[Anvil]] and the counter-UAS stack

## Where the money comes from

Anchor programs include the [[Army Enterprise Contract]] ($20B, March 2026), [[IVAS]] (up to $22B program taken over from Microsoft), and the [[CCA Program]] with the U.S. Air Force. Production scales through [[Arsenal-1]] — see [[Manufacturing Strategy]].
