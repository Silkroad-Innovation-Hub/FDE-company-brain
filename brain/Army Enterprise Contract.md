# Army Enterprise Contract ($20B, March 2026)

In March 2026 the U.S. Army awarded [[Anduril]] a landmark **$20 billion enterprise contract** consolidating **120+ existing technology orders** into one vehicle, centering [[Lattice]] as the backbone of the Army's **counter-drone defense architecture**.

## Why it matters

- Converts scattered pilots into a durable program of record — the core of the 2026 [[Revenue]] step-up.
- Scales the counter-UAS stack ([[Anvil]], [[Roadrunner]], sentry towers, Pulsar EW) across installations.
- Validates the land-and-expand model: hardware wedges in, [[Lattice]] consolidates.

Paired with [[IVAS]], it makes the Army Anduril's largest customer.
