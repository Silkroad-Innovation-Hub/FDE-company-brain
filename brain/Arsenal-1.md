# Arsenal-1

Arsenal-1 is [[Anduril]]'s **$1B, 5-million-square-foot factory** near Rickenbacker International Airport in Pickaway County, Ohio — the physical proof of [[Manufacturing Strategy]].

## Status (mid-2026)

- **[[Fury]] serial production began March 2026, ~3 months ahead of the original July 2026 target.**
- [[Roadrunner]], [[Barracuda]], and one classified platform slated to join by end of 2026.
- Designed around commercial automotive-style lines: high-rate, multi-product, software-configured.

Arsenal-1 is the answer to the standard critique of defense startups — "you can't build at scale." The [[Series H]] raise explicitly funds its expansion.
