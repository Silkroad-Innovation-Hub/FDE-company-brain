# Roadrunner

Roadrunner is a **reusable, twin-jet, VTOL autonomous interceptor** unveiled in 2023. Its signature trick: launch, hunt, and — if it doesn't engage — fly home, land vertically, and be reused.

- Variant **Roadrunner-M** carries a warhead for counter-UAS and cruise-missile defense.
- Controlled through [[Lattice]]; pairs with [[Anvil]] and sentry towers in the layered base-defense stack.
- Production is slated to join [[Fury]] at [[Arsenal-1]] by end of 2026.

Roadrunner reframes air defense economics: the interceptor costs less than many of the threats it kills, and you get it back.
