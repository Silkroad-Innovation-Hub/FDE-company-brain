# Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA)

The U.S. Air Force's CCA program pairs crewed fighters with autonomous wingmen. [[Anduril]]'s [[Fury]] (YFQ-44A) was selected in 2024 for Increment 1 alongside General Atomics — the two-horse race that put a startup in the fighter-aircraft business.

- Flight testing progressed through 2025; **serial production started at [[Arsenal-1]] in March 2026, ahead of schedule**.
- Autonomy stack is [[Lattice]] Mission Autonomy.
- Rheinmetall extends the Fury line to Europe — see [[Partnerships]].

CCA is the existence proof for [[Manufacturing Strategy]]: fighter-class capability, munition-class economics.
