# Manufacturing Strategy

[[Anduril]]'s industrial thesis: **the constraint on Western defense is production rate, not invention.**

1. Design for manufacture — commercial components, few exotic parts ([[Barracuda]] is the extreme case).
2. Own the factory — [[Arsenal-1]]: $1B, 5M sq ft, multi-product lines.
3. Software-defined everything — one [[Lattice]] build, many airframes; hardware iterates like firmware.
4. Prove rate early — [[Fury]] serial production started **3 months ahead of schedule** (March 2026).
5. Fund with private capital first ([[Funding History]]), sell working systems, not proposals.

The bet: whoever wins the rate war wins the re-armament decade. See [[Company Timeline]].
