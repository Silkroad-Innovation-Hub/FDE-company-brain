# Partnerships

- **Meta** — [[EagleEye]] mixed-reality hardware; consumer supply chain for military wearables.
- **Microsoft** — ceded [[IVAS]] operational leadership (Feb 2025), remains cloud/AI partner.
- **OpenAI** — AI models for national-security missions, incl. counter-UAS decision support feeding [[Lattice]].
- **Rheinmetall** — European channel for [[Fury]] and [[Barracuda]].
- **Royal Australian Navy** — [[Ghost Shark]] co-development; AUKUS anchor.
- **Palantir** — data/software interoperability consortium for DoD programs.

Pattern: partner where distribution or supply chain is the bottleneck; compete where autonomy is the product — see [[Competitors]].
