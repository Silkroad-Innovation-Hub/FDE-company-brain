# Fury (YFQ-44A)

Fury is [[Anduril]]'s **collaborative combat aircraft** — a jet-powered autonomous fighter designed to fly alongside crewed aircraft as a loyal wingman under the U.S. Air Force [[CCA Program]].

## Key facts

- Designation **YFQ-44A**; originated at Blue Force Technologies (acquired 2023 — see [[Acquisitions]]).
- **Serial production began at [[Arsenal-1]] in March 2026, roughly three months ahead of schedule** — a deliberate proof point for [[Manufacturing Strategy]].
- Flown and coordinated through [[Lattice]] mission autonomy.
- Rheinmetall partnership extends Fury (and [[Barracuda]]) to European customers — see [[Partnerships]].

Fury is the flagship argument that software-defined aircraft can be built at fighter-jet capability but munition-like cost and rate.
