# Ghost

Ghost is [[Anduril]]'s family of small **autonomous helicopter drones** for intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR).

- Man-packable, near-silent, launches in minutes.
- Feeds full-motion video and sensor data straight into [[Lattice]].
- Ghost-X extends endurance and payload; used alongside [[Altius]] in expeditionary kits.
- Combat-proven including in Ukraine deployments.

Ghost is often the first Anduril product a customer touches — cheap to adopt, and it pulls [[Lattice]] in the door.
