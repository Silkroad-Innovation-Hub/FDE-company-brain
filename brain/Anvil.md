# Anvil

Anvil is a **kinetic counter-drone interceptor**: a quadcopter that rams hostile drones out of the sky, cued automatically by [[Lattice]] from sentry-tower and radar tracks.

- **Anvil-M** adds a munition payload for harder targets.
- Sits in the layered counter-UAS stack with [[Roadrunner]], Pulsar EW, and sentry towers.
- Core to the counter-drone architecture the [[Army Enterprise Contract]] scales across bases.

Anvil made the case early that autonomy — not human joystick piloting — is the only way base defense scales.
