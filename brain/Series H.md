# Series H — $5B at $61B (June 2026)

On June 15, 2026, [[Anduril]] closed a **$5 billion Series H** led by **Thrive Capital and Andreessen Horowitz**, doubling the valuation to **$61 billion** twelve months after the [[Series G]].

## Use of proceeds

- Expand [[Arsenal-1]] and additional production capacity
- Scale [[IVAS]]/[[EagleEye]] delivery and the [[Army Enterprise Contract]]
- International growth — see [[Partnerships]]

Valuation has roughly doubled every year since 2022 — full arc in [[Funding History]]; revenue support in [[Revenue]]. IPO speculation is persistent but unconfirmed.
