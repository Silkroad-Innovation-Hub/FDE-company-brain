# IVAS → Soldier-Borne Mission Command

In **February 2025** [[Anduril]] took over operational leadership of the U.S. Army's **Integrated Visual Augmentation System (IVAS)** — a program worth **up to $22 billion** — from Microsoft, converting a troubled headset effort into the [[EagleEye]] product line.

- Microsoft remains a cloud/AI partner; Meta supplies MR hardware DNA — see [[Partnerships]].
- [[Palmer Luckey]]'s personal arc: consumer VR founder returns to fix military MR.
- Soldier headsets become [[Lattice]] nodes — every soldier a sensor.

IVAS is the highest-profile example of Anduril inheriting and rescuing a prime-led program.
